## Notes on wording

- **No drug or brand names.** "These medicines" refers to GLP-1 medicines as a class. The trials cited studied semaglutide.
- **No overstating.** The regain finding came from people who stopped the medicine *and* the trial's lifestyle programme, and the card says exactly that.
- **Rounding.** "About 10%" and "about 19%" are rounded from 9.7% and 19.3%. These are changes from each person's starting lean mass and fat mass, not shares of the total weight lost.
- **Muscle vs lean mass.** DEXA "lean mass" includes water and organ tissue as well as muscle. The card says "lean mass" when quoting the trial, and "muscle" only for the protein and strength advice.
- **Not medical advice.** Every screen carries "General education, not medical advice", and the handoff sends people to a doctor, not to a product.
